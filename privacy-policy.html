Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mengedoht CNC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website, mengedohtcnc.com.

Information We Collect

If you submit our contact form, we collect what you enter: name, email address, and optionally phone number, business name, and your message or project details. If you leave a review on the site, we collect your name, optional role or location, star rating, review text, and an optional photo. We use this information only to respond to you, fulfill your request, or publish the review you submitted, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it to third parties.

If you email or call us directly using the contact links on this site, that conversation happens between you and Mengedoht CNC, not through this website.

Cookies and Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors arrived here. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information like your approximate location, device and browser type, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and does not include the personal information you might submit through our contact form. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Third-Party Services

This website relies on a few third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — used to display the typefaces on this site. Loading these fonts may share your browser's IP address with Google, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics — used to measure site traffic, as described above.
  • Formspree — processes submissions from our contact form and delivers them to us by email. Formspree may retain submitted data subject to its own privacy policy.
  • Stripe — some products link out to Stripe's own checkout page for payment. We do not receive or store your payment card details; that transaction is handled entirely by Stripe under its own privacy policy.
  • Instagram — our Gallery page may display recent posts from our Instagram account, and other pages link out to Instagram. Viewing embedded posts or following those links is subject to Instagram's (Meta's) own privacy policy, not ours.
  • Cloudflare — this website is hosted on Cloudflare, which may log standard technical information (like IP addresses) for security and performance purposes.

We don't control these third parties' privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies if you have questions.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Privacy Rights

If you'd like us to access, correct, or delete information you've shared with us, for example through the contact form or a review, just let us know and we'll take care of it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website or business practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
