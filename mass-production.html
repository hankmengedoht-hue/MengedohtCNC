What to Expect

Minimum Order Quantity

There's no minimum for one-off custom parts. For a production run of your own part design, tiers start at 25 units — contact us to scope your specific project.

Lead Times

Generally a 3-week lead time — often completed sooner depending on the job.

Payment Terms

For new accounts, payment is required at time of order.

Shipping

Local delivery in North Charleston, SC is available. We ship via UPS and FedEx Ground. Freight available for large orders. All products are securely packaged for transit. Damage claims are handled promptly — we stand behind every shipment.
