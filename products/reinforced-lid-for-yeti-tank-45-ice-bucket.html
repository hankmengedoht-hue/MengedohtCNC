Upgrade your Yeti Tank 45 with a reinforced replacement lid built to outperform the stock lid in every way that matters. This lid is constructed from marine grade, hard plastic materials designed to handle saltwater exposure, daily impact, and constant outdoor use without warping or cracking over time. The reinforced design delivers a noticeably better fit than the original stock lid, sealing more securely around the rim so it stays locked in place even when the bucket is full or under load. It also holds significantly more weight than the stock lid, making it a smart choice for anglers, boaters, and anyone who uses their Yeti Tank 45 as a step, seat, or platform on the boat or dock. The added structural reinforcement means you can rely on this lid for tasks the original was never built to handle. Insulation performance is also improved, with the lid providing better temperature retention than the stock version to help keep ice frozen longer and contents colder for extended trips. Its UV resistant finish protects against sun damage, preventing the fading, yellowing, and brittleness that often affects lids left exposed to the elements day after day. Further, the optional foam pad on the top improves grip. Built with anglers in mind, the lid includes a dedicated notch that allows a fishing bubbler cord to pass through cleanly, so you can run an aerator directly into the bucket and keep bait alive and lively for longer without having to leave the lid propped open or unsealed. Whether you're out on the water, hauling gear to a job site, or setting up for a long day of fishing, this reinforced lid gives your Yeti Tank 45 the strength, seal, and insulation it needs to keep performing at its best.

The Story Behind This Product

This lid was developed out of frustration with stock lids that seal poorly, crack under weight, and let bait die faster on long days out. It was built to solve those problems directly, with a tighter seal, added strength, and a bubbler notch that keeps bait alive and lively for longer.
